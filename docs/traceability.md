## Current Checked-In Stage Summary

`results/stage_summary_table.csv` reports:

| Stage | Samples | Mean proxy | Mean white-region fraction |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| post_defoliation | 265 | 9586.962264 | 0.031344693 |
| pre_defoliation | 160 | 4564.831250 | 0.017528029 |

These values support the paper's qualitative claim that post-defoliation imagery
has higher visible cotton proxy values and larger white-region fraction. They do
not exactly reproduce the paper's draft Table II.
